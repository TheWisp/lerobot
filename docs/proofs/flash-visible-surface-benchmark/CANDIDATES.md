# Candidate feasibility and comparison boundary

The [protocol](../../../src/lerobot/policies/flash/docs/visible_surface_benchmark.md) lists point frontends and complete pose systems. The completed raw-point tables cover ten frontends on the moving holdout (TAPIP3D's filtered/unfiltered depth paths and SpaTrackerV2's native/sensor-depth paths are ablations of one frontend each). They do **not** yet constitute an evaluation of every complete system or a production tracker selection.

The additional public DexYCB sample is visually useful but has neither material-point ground truth nor source timestamps. A second-source accuracy claim needs independent point/pose annotation or another labelled dataset. The [local moving yellow-block and gamepad clips](LOCAL_MOVING.md), like the dowel recording, cannot by themselves identify the correct material points. A segmentation mask, measured depth and a rigid fit can reject some implausible predictions, but they cannot substitute for those identity labels.

Measured model and adapter times exclude shared SAM, correspondence validation, common rigid fitting, FLASH planning, camera/motor transfer, recording and display. The [common fit](FIT_MOTION.md) adds about 2 ms on the short clips. A real 30 Hz decision requires the integrated asynchronous perception path and independent robot consumer under simultaneous load, plus the age of each published estimate. No full-clip model is granted a causal update rate merely because its average offline throughput exceeds 30 fps.
