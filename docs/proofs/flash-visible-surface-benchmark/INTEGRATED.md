# Paced tracker, mask and fit overhead

Three independent process runs per mode on the same 91-frame, approximately 30 Hz local dowel clip. All modes used the same TAPNext checkpoint as the labelled point-tracking comparison and 64 first-frame queries. The consumer is a separate 30 Hz Python thread; the producer processes the latest available source frame and publishes only after the selected stages finish. Live SAM3 is point-seeded on the first frame at 504 px and then propagated. This measures one serialized perception path, not the full FLASH robot or concurrent segmentation and point-tracking processes. The common rigid fit uses measured depth and 10 mm RANSAC residuals; its availability is not a pose-accuracy score.

The mask/tracker/fit times exclude model loading but include per-frame work; first-frame model warmup is included in skipped-frame and age counts. All fit and depth stages run after tracking. A ticking consumer can still act on an old estimate: the live-mask mode's p90 age is much larger than its 33 ms tick period. No motor IO, camera capture, recording, display, planner or independent point-identity truth is part of this timing probe. Mask correctness and fit correctness must be evaluated separately; nonempty masks and available fits are not accuracy evidence. Both modes with a fit produced one on every processed frame. The slower live-mask schedule skips source frames and therefore feeds a different sequence into the temporal tracker; comparing their pose errors would require scoring those actual processed frames against independent truth.
