# Paced tracker, mask and fit overhead

Three independent process runs per mode on the same 91-frame, approximately 30 Hz local dowel clip. All modes used the same TAPNext checkpoint and 64 first-frame queries. The consumer is a separate 30 Hz Python thread; the producer processes the latest available source frame and publishes only after the selected stages finish. Live SAM3 is point-seeded on the first frame at 504 px and then propagated. This measures one serialized perception path, not the full FLASH robot or concurrent segmentation and point-tracking processes. The common rigid fit uses measured depth and 10 mm RANSAC residuals; its availability is not a pose-accuracy score.

The mask/tracker/fit times exclude model loading but include per-frame work; first-frame model warmup is included in skipped-frame and age counts. All fit and depth stages run after tracking. A ticking consumer can still act on an old estimate: the live-mask mode's p90 age is much larger than its 33 ms tick period. No motor IO, camera capture, recording, display, planner or independent point-identity truth is part of this timing probe. Mask correctness and fit correctness must be evaluated separately; nonempty masks and available fits are not accuracy evidence. The cached path's fit cost is near zero on many frames because the point frontend has fewer than three visible points with valid measured depth there, so no 3D fit is possible. The slower live-mask schedule skips source frames and therefore feeds a different sequence into the temporal tracker; its higher fit availability must not be attributed to better masks.
